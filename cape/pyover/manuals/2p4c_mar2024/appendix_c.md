## **Appendix C. Boundary Condition Types**

Specific application details for many of the boundary condition types are included in Chapter 3. Boundary condition numbers have a superscripted symbol that indicates if they have been linearized for use with ILHS=26 or 27. Those that have been linearized have a +, approximately linearized have a \*, and those that have not been linearized have a -.
